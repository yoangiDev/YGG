## Lessons

### When a trade leaves you low, leave their range before you do anything else

Three of your four deaths followed a lost trade almost at once. At 8:45 the trade left you at 20% and Rell at 11%, and at 8:51 Nautilus, Graves and Lucian killed you in a 3v3 with a summoner spell still up (300 gold, 16 s dead). At 10:52 you were at 1% and Rell had burned Flash and Heal, and you died at 10:57 (231 gold, 20 s dead, your side 2,780 gold behind in items). At 12:15 you were at 9% and died at 12:19 in a 1v2.

**Fix:** As soon as a trade leaves you under 25%, step out of their reach (behind the wave, toward your tower) and use the summoner spell before the bar is gone if you can't. Staying to finish a trade you are losing is the death.

### Don't trade into a wave that is not yours

You won one trade (5:25, Lucian at 6%) and lost four: at 8:26 you took 0.0 health bars off them and gave up 0.5, at 8:45 you took 0.9 and gave 1.1, at 10:52 0.8 and 1.1, and at 12:15 0.4 and 1.4. Added up you took 2.1 bars and gave 4.1. In every one of the four the wave barely moved or rolled back into you, so the damage you gave up bought you nothing; in the one you won, you gave up none.

**Fix:** Take a trade only when the wave is moving toward their tower or you can finish it with a kill. Against a wave that is coming back, last-hit and let it stack.

### Count who nobody has seen before you stand in the lane

Three of your four deaths came from an enemy nobody on your team had seen for over 30 seconds: Lucian for 36 s at 3:52 (a 2v2, 300 gold given), Lucian again for 36 s at 8:51, and Nautilus for 38 s at 10:57. The 3:52 one came at the very end of your first three-wave slow push (2:35 to 3:55).

**Fix:** When Lucian or Nautilus has been out of sight for 30 seconds, play next to your tower or behind the wave and make them come to you.
