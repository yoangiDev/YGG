## What this doesn't know

- **Whether there really was a choice.** Sometimes the fight finds you, and the state doesn't see the wave, the cooldowns or the
  vision of each fight.
- **Associations, not experiments.** Whoever enters at a disadvantage may have a reason that isn't seen here. That is why it is
  read as "what usually happens in that state", not as "what would have happened".
- **Swaps between objectives** (grubs for dragon) are not in the trees.
- **The policy imitates the high league**, not what wins. For the recalls, the next step is measuring their value in WP.
