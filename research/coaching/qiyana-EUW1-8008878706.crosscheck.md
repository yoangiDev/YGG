## Answers and what was implemented (`research/replays/jungle.py`)

1. **Advantage (snowball):** it isn't the gold carried but the levels (one is enough), the gold in items and the quality of the items, and the state of the map:
   lanes winning with a good wave and who arrives first (distance between movement speeds). → `advantage()`: a level of difference with the enemy jungler, or
   +600 g in items, or three lanes ahead with +300 g in items. Each text says which one was met.
2. **Recall window:** the gold, the teammates' tempo and the objective timers (dragon, voids, herald or an enemy camp that matters). → `recall_windows()`:
   with ≥1,300 g on them, away from base, and going back more than 30 s late without having gotten anything out of the time; the importance rises to HIGH with teammates going back at the same time,
   an objective in play and an advantage. The recall that is made comes out good ("late but right" if it wasn't the first window).
3. **Net result of the play:** deaths, half-done camps and stolen camps INSIDE a play or fight that paid off are not separate mistakes. → `net_of_plays()`:
   isolates the window (difference of the differences between each position and its opponent, without the earlier advantage), lowers the tone to neutral and gives the numbers per position.
   Applies to 3:43, 3:53 and 6:13; in the dragon, what it is worth with the objective is also quoted.
4. **With an advantage and an objective in play**, opening to camps is suboptimal (9:24 → bad-low; 10:47 → neutral, the Dragon comes out 10 s later); and cancelling a recall with
   an objective less than a minute away and an advantage is bad (14:25, and the "far from the Herald" of 14:11 becomes bad).
5. **The jungler's level 1 wards:** the eight spots with your names (Raptors entrance, river banana bush, Enemy Raptors, Enemy Blue) with a radius of 800 u; outside
   them, neutral.
6. **Upgraded smite:** the pet (1 charge per large monster + 1 per stored charge, one every 60 s; 15 for the first evolution). The Gromp at 5:17 comes out
   as the 15th charge and with the Smite spent on it right before the crab at 5:41 and with the Dragon in play. Assumption: the stored charges start counting
   on buying the item.
7. **First clear:** any champion, the sooner the better; it warns that some can't but that it is still the goal.
