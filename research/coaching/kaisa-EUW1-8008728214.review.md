## Lessons

### Count the enemies you have not seen before you walk forward

Six of your eleven deaths in this game came from an enemy the team had lost: Nami at 15:00 (44 s unseen), Corki at 21:57 (25 s), Nami again at 25:15 and 33:42 (1:11 each), Locke at 28:18 (you never saw him) and Viego at 35:38 (35 s). They were spread over bot lane (15:00, 33:42), mid lane (21:57, 35:38), your top jungle (25:15) and the top side river (28:18), and each cost you 28-58 seconds dead and 300-650 gold of farm. At 21:57 and at 28:18 the enemy took Baron while you were dead. The 15:00 death followed your sidelane opening at 14:16, which had already cost you Flash at 14:59.

**Fix:** Before you step forward into a lane, mid or the river, name the enemies you have not seen for 30+ seconds. If one of them has been gone that long, stay next to vision or your tower and let them come to you.

### When your team is ahead, do not be the first one in

At 17:40 your team was 62% to win and 4,020 gold ahead. At 17:38 you did 88 damage in a 5v3 in the bot side river, and at 17:43 you were the first to fall (350 gold given, 36 s dead). The enemy then killed seven of your players for two, took the Chemtech Drake and, at 17:49, 1,927 health off your mid outer turret. Your win chance was 21% by 19:18. The sequence that turned the game started with you in the river and the turret went with you.

**Fix:** In a river fight with a lead, take the first hit last: let someone else open it and join from behind your front line, with your summoners up.

### Recall when the wave is pushed, and spend your gold and summoners before you walk forward

At 13:45 your wave was pushing into their tower, which is your recall timer, and you stayed in the lane: you died alone with a summoner spell up (330 gold, 28 s dead). At 24:11 you died in the bot side river carrying 1,686 gold, and at 31:11 you died with 2,027 gold and both summoner spells still up. In each of those deaths something you owned went unused.

**Fix:** After you push the wave, recall straight away. With more than 1,500 gold in your pocket, go to the shop before you walk forward, and use your summoners in a fight you are going to lose.
