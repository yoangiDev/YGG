# Qiyana · EUW1-8008878706 · jungle routes

The route of each decision of the Qiyana in the review (`qiyana-EUW1-8008878706.author.md`), against what the ladder's junglers
do. It comes from the second version of the prototype (`research/replays/reports/routes.md`): **no hand-written rules**,
everything learned from 256,092 jungle decisions in 5,264 games.

## How to read it

There is one decision at the start (the first camp) and another after each camp, crab or objective until 14:00.
For each one:

- **High league**: the routes that at least 5% of the Master-and-above junglers would choose in that same state
  (level, health, gold, waves, where the enemy jungler was seen…), with the share choosing each one. It only uses what the
  player could know.
- **In parentheses**: what that route is worth against the other decisions in the same state, in neutral WP points
  **at 90 s / 3 min / 5 min** (a kill is worth about 4.7), and how much the risk of dying within 90 s goes up or down.
  **≈** means the 90% interval crosses zero: it can't be told apart from any route in that state.
- **The state** it is compared in is the moment; from the 3rd camp on, also whether the enemy jungler is going to
  gank and where (given by the heading: "the enemy jungler is going to gank mid"); from the 7th on, also the health and unspent
  gold. For ganks, if the enemy isn't going to gank, according to that lane's wave ("enemy ahead", "with prio").
  It is an after-the-game analysis, so the value does use what she didn't know at that moment.
- **Did**: what she did.

With 5,000 games, the value state by state still isn't learned (see `routes.md`): it is that of the state's cell,
not of its exact second. That is why almost everything comes out with ≈ in a single decision, and what is clearly seen is little: the
objective, the crab at half health, going back to base with nothing to buy, ganking with prio.

## What this doesn't know

- **Whether the counter-gank was on purpose**: it is marked by where the enemy jungler was, not by whether she expected it. Her
  team hadn't seen him in the 10 s before; if she deduced it from the camps (tracking), that doesn't enter.
- **"Objective" always comes out in favour** because it carries the objective inside: it is good for seeing that it was chosen, not for
  comparing it with the others.
- **At 5 min what she did afterwards enters**, not only the route.
- **Association, not experiment**: whoever chooses a route may know something the state doesn't see.
