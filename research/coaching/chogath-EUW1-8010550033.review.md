## Lessons

### From minute 22, count the champions on each side before you step in

The four deaths in the last third of the game began with fewer champions on your side: 4v5 at 22:42 in your top-side jungle (370 gold, 43 s dead), 1v2 at 25:36 in mid lane with Flash still up (380 gold, 46 s), 4v5 at 30:04 in the Raptors bush of their jungle (390 gold, 51 s, win chance 41% to 27%, the costliest of your six deaths, carrying 2,479 unspent gold) and 3v4 at 33:04 in mid lane (410 gold, 57 s). That last one fell inside the swing from 72% at 32:15 to 43% at 33:06, with your team losing 2 champions and killing none and team gold going from -170 to -2,171.

**Fix:** Before you start or join a fight, count both sides. With fewer champions than them, stay with your team or behind the wave and step in only when the numbers are even. And with a full item in your pocket (2,479 gold at 30:04), recall first: the gold is worth nothing dead.

### Before an objective, keep your Flash and be at it when it spawns

At 9:46 you fought in your bot-side jungle with the Gromp and Wolves up: the fight itself netted your side +552 gold and Rell died, but they took the Dragon out of it (about 1,200 gold in win chance) and the window ended at -580 gold with the objective counted. At 19:34 you flashed in a 2v2 in the bot-side river and died (360 gold, 39 s dead) with the next Dragon 1:36 away: Flash takes 300 s to come back, so you would have reached that Dragon without it. At 30:59 you reached the pit 4 s after the Dragon spawned at 31:44 and your team took it 43 s after that.

**Fix:** Look at the Dragon and Voidgrub timers before you commit to a fight, keep Flash for the 45 seconds around a spawn, and be at the objective when it spawns, with vision placed and looking for a pick nearby.

### Close the camps Quinn can reach before he takes them

Quinn took your Wolves at 4:09 (85 gold), your Krugs at 5:46 (109 gold) and your Gromp at 7:19 (80 gold): 274 gold that went to him instead of you, and each camp he takes you can't clear for two more minutes. **Fix:** Clear the Raptors and the Krugs in the same pass, and when you have not seen Quinn for a minute, take first the camps he would reach before you and ward the entrance of the side you are leaving.
