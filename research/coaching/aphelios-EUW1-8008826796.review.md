Three of your seven deaths came from an enemy nobody on your team had seen for 30+ seconds: Lillia at 13:49 (32 s), Shen at 28:43 (37 s, out of the fog 11 s before he killed you) and Ahri at 30:09 (1:15). **Fix:** After you deal your damage in a fight, name the enemies who are missing before you follow it into the river. If one has been gone 30+ seconds, stay with your team's vision and use the summoner spell before you fall.

**Fix:** Leave base for mid with your team, and if you take a sidelane, collect one wave and go mid. When you are the item-behind side, do not take a 1v1 in mid.

**Fix:** When you push the wave, press recall at once. Take a trade only if you can finish it; otherwise last-hit, move away from the casts and let the wave come to you.
