## Lessons

### Recall as soon as the wave is pushed, and count who you have not seen

At 10:17 your wave was already pushing into their tower, which is the moment to recall, and you stayed in the lane. Qiyana killed you without you seeing her, with a summoner spell still up: 310 gold given and 20 s dead, while they took the top outer turret. At 11:54 it was the same kind of mistake: nobody on your team had seen Gragas for 44 s, and he came out of the fog 7 s before the 2v1 that killed you (320 gold, 25 s dead). The plate trade at 12:25, 3 for 1, happened while you were dead.

**Fix:** After pushing the wave, press recall at once. Before you move forward, count the enemies you have not seen: if it has been 30-40 s for one of them, stay near vision or your tower.

### Open mid, and keep Flash for the objective

At 19:10 you opened to bot from base. You were caught there and spent your Flash at 19:19, with the dragon 23 s away; Flash takes 300 s to come back, so you reached the dragon without it. You also opened to bot at 14:49. In this game the opening that worked was mid: at 12:24 Locke was covering bot, so you took the mid wave and only one wave was lost instead of two, and at 13:00 you swapped to mid after the first tower.

**Fix:** From base, go mid with your team. If you take a sidelane, collect one wave and go mid, and do not spend Flash when the next objective is under a minute away.

### Every minion under tower and every unanswered hit is gold

At 2:19 a cannon wave of theirs crashed under your tower: 7 minions entered its range, you took 5 and 2 died without anyone on your side collecting them, about 42 gold. In lane you also took 8 enemy ability hits without answering: 633 health in total, about 4 potions. The first was 90 health at 1:28, from Xayah's Q while you went for a minion.

**Fix:** Predict when the tower takes aggro and time your last hit to it. When they cast at you while you farm, move on the cast and punish them while they are walking up for the minion.
