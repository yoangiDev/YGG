Map zones

The list is the priority: the first zone that contains a point names it.

How to edit
  • Pick a shape (rectangle, ellipse, triangle, diamond) and drag on the map, or use Polygon and click the corners (double-click, Enter or the first point closes it, Esc cancels).
  • Then select the zone and drag its white dots to reshape it; double-click a dot to remove the corner; click a dark dot (middle of an edge) to add one.
  • The eye hides a zone on the map only (it still names places). ▲ ▼ change the priority.
  • A zone with a side reads "your …" for that team and "the enemy …" for the other.
Move the mouse over the map.
